# Release Changelog v1.2.0

*Released on: Saturday, 3 October 2026*

**Catalyst is now part of Essentials. Training, Fuel and an AI coach join hydration, spend and check-in in one app — and every write is now instant, saved on the phone first and synced to the cloud in the background.**

### ✨ New Features

- **Train tab** — Today's session from your weekly split: a big done-count with a segment rail, total load and streak readouts, and every movement as an expandable row with its coaching cue, a load stepper pre-filled from last time, and a "Watch form" link. Finish the session to log it.
- **Week plan** — See the whole split laid out on this week's dates, open any day, and "Train this today" to shift the plan. A morning/evening check-in slides the week back a day when you skip, so nothing gets missed. The shift resets every Monday.
- **Import your own split** — Hand the coach a PDF, a photo or pasted text of any program and it becomes your weekly split (synced to your account). One tap goes back to the default.
- **Muscles view** — Anatomy plates for the muscles today's session targets.
- **Fuel tab** — The 28-day meal plan as a 4 × 7 cycle field that brightens as you log meals. Mark each meal eaten, swapped (with a note) or skipped; browse back and ahead; realign the cycle to any day.
- **Restock list** — On restock days (plan days 1, 8, 15, 22) your grocery list is ready. Ticking an item logs it to Spend as Groceries; unticking removes it — one ledger, not two.
- **Meal scan** — Photograph a plate for a calorie estimate, protein/carbs/fat split, a 1–10 score and one line of advice.
- **Coach** — A chat that knows today's session, meals, water and your body metrics. Pick Flash, Flash Lite or Pro; each has a daily budget you can see.
- **Session read-out** — On-demand coach feedback on today's session, with one specific progressive-overload move for next time.
- **Training & meal reminders** — 06:00 wake-up and 07:00 "time to train" on training days, meal-time nudges and a restock-day reminder, on their own notification channel and sound. Each can be switched off in Profile.
- **Body metrics** — Weight, target, height and age in Profile, with BMI and kg-to-target.
- **App lock** — Optional fingerprint/face lock for the whole app (re-locks after 5 minutes away). Never covers a ringing alarm.

### 🎨 UI & UX Modernization

- **Four-tab nav** — Home · Train · Fuel · Profile in the same hairline pill.
- **Today's plan on Home** — Training and Fuel progress sit under the Spend/Check-in cards as two quiet columns.
- **Profile rebuilt** — Your name as the headline and a column of clear rows (check-in, alarm, version, sync) instead of coloured tiles. The Discord setup is now a bottom sheet.
- **One shared bottom sheet** — Every new flow uses the same draggable sheet, so they all move and dismiss the same way.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

- **Saving is instant now** — Water, spend, UPI, profile and all new data save on the phone first and upload in the background. Before, every tap waited on a network check and the server.
- **Offline changes now actually reach the cloud** — Changes made offline were queued but never uploaded. They now sync automatically, on app open, and on a retry timer; Profile shows how many are waiting.
- **"Clear today's water" no longer risks older days** — An offline clear could wipe your whole water history once synced. It now only ever removes that day's logs.
- **Barcode alarm and gallery QR scanning are safe for future builds** — Their native code only existed on the old build machine and would have been lost on the next clean build. It has been recovered and is now part of the project.

### 🧹 Cleanup

- Firebase config falls back to the Essentials `google-services.json` when `.env` is missing.
- New docs: `README.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `RULES.md`, `docs/CATALYST_MERGE.md`.
