# Release Changelog v1.4.0

*Released on: Wednesday, 7 October 2026*

**Train gets a muscle explorer, an exercise library, set-by-set logging, records, goals and a weekly review — everything useful from the Forma gym app, now inside Essentials.**

### 🧍 Explore

- **Muscle map** — Tap any of 14 muscles on a front/back body to see what it does and which moves train it.
- **Exercise library** — 34 moves with a picture, step-by-step how-to, breathing, common mistakes and an alternative. Filter by equipment and level, search, and star your favourites.
- **Add to a day** — Put any move into your split straight from its page.

### 🏋️ Train

- **Set by set** — Log reps and kilograms for every set. "Use last time" fills your previous numbers, and the − / + buttons change all the sets you haven't done yet.
- **Rest timer** — A 60-second ring starts when you tick a set.
- **Muscles sheet** — Today's muscles glow on the body map instead of the old photo plates.
- **Edit your split** — Rename a day, reorder moves, change sets and reps, or add moves from the library.

### 📈 Progress

- **7 or 30 days** — Sessions, minutes, sets and kilograms moved, where the work went on the body, and sets per day.
- **Records** — Your heaviest set and estimated max for every move, with a confetti-worthy "new record" on the finish screen.
- **Goals** — Set a target for sessions, minutes or active days and watch the ring fill.
- **Weekly review** — One win, what got in the way, your next step and how confident you feel.
