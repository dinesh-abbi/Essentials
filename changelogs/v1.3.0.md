# Release Changelog v1.3.0

*Released on: Sunday, 4 October 2026*

**A brand-new look for the whole app, made to be read at a glance — pictures, rings and colours first, words last. Inspired by Apple's rings, Samsung One UI's big titles and Nothing's dot-matrix style. The AI coach is gone.**

### 🎨 New Design — "Glance"

- **One colour per area** — Water is cyan, Train is coral, Fuel is lime, Spend is sunshine, Check-in is lavender, the alarm is pink. The same colour shows up on Home, in its tab and on every screen of that area.
- **Chunky buttons and tiles** — Everything you can press looks pressable and sinks when tapped.
- **Dot-matrix numbers** — Big counts, times and dates use a dot-matrix font. Words use a friendly rounded font.
- **New tab bar** — Icons with labels, each tab in its own colour, with a springy highlight.

### 🏠 Home, Train, Fuel, You

- **Today's rings on Home** — Water, meals and training rings close as your day fills up.
- **Meet Drip** — The water drop fills up as you drink and its face goes from thirsty to cheering. Tap Drip to log a glass.
- **Bento tiles** — Train, Fuel, Spend and Check-in each show a picture, one number and a tiny visual.
- **Train** — A ring that fills as you tick moves off, the week as seven circles, and a big check button on every move.
- **Fuel** — The 28-day plan as a ring, the month as a dot field, and each meal answered with one tap: Ate · Swap · Skip.
- **You** — Big centred profile, settings with coloured icons, body stats as picture tiles.

### 💧 Water

- **Day / Week / Month** with a sliding switch. Day shows Drip big, three quick-add buttons and an hour-by-hour dot row.
- **Week** as chunky bars with a dashed goal line; **Month** as a calendar of dots that glow brighter the more you drank.
- **Goal hit!** — Confetti and a cheering Drip when you reach your goal.
- Setting your goal is now a sheet with one-tap presets.

### 💰 Spend

- **Today's total big**, a little donut of what you spent on, and one **Add expense** button.
- **Every category has a picture and a colour**; expenses are grouped by day.
- **Adding/editing** is one sheet: giant amount, name, date and time, and a picture grid of categories.
- **Week bars** and a **month category donut** with coloured bars for each category.
- **UPI pay** — Big amount, picture categories, and a lively scanner with a moving scan line.

### 📸 Check-in, ⏰ Alarm and more

- **Check-in camera** — Lavender viewfinder, live clock, one huge shutter, friendly progress steps. If there's no signal the same photo is saved and sent later.
- **Alarm setup** — A three-step checklist (time, sound, barcode) that ticks as you go, with a giant clock you tap to change.
- **Ringing alarm** — Huge pulsing clock, pink viewfinder and a countdown ring to the snooze.
- **Sign-in, app lock, Discord setup, What's new and Update** screens all redesigned to match.

### 🧹 Removed

- **AI coach chat, meal photo scan and AI program import** — taken out completely. No API key is needed any more.

### 🐛 Fixes

- The "goal reached" notification now uses your own goal instead of the default 3 L.
- A failed check-in upload saves the photo you just took instead of taking a second one.
